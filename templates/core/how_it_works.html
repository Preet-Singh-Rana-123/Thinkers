FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I create a club for my specific college?

Yes! Anyone can launch an Entrepreneur Club. You can make it university-specific (e.g. "IIT Delhi Blockchain Club") or global across multiple universities.

Is this platform completely free for students?

100% free! Thinkers is built to democratize student entrepreneurship. You can join unlimited clubs, post pitches, and attend mastermind calls for free.

What if I don't have a startup idea yet?

Even better! Join clubs as an "Aspiring Hustler" or "Hacker". You can contribute to ongoing open pitches, join as a co-founder, or participate in monthly brainstorm sprints.

Who are the mentors and judges?

Our mentor network includes verified alumni founders, university innovation officers, and angel syndicate partners who volunteer hours for college clubs.
